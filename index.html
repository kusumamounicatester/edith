Project lifecycle

How a project moves through EDITH

Nine stages, one accountable team. Select a stage to see what happens, who is responsible, and what EDITH's role is at each step.

Land identification & title check

A parcel is identified and evaluated — title, zoning, FSI, and access are checked before anything is committed. JV or land-owner conversations begin here.

Led by: Land & JV team

EDITH's role

  • Title due diligence
  • Zoning & FSI check
  • JV structure proposal

Feasibility & financial model

Cost, saleable area, and likely returns are modelled before a single rupee of construction spend is approved. The break-even rate and investor return scenarios are fixed here.

Led by: CA/CS-led finance team

EDITH's role

  • Construction cost model
  • Break-even analysis
  • Investor return scenarios

Architectural & structural design

Plans are drawn balancing unit mix, cost, and buildability. The design is optimised for the saleable area efficiency EDITH needs to hit the feasibility model's targets.

Led by: Design & engineering team

EDITH's role

  • Architect coordination
  • Unit mix optimisation
  • Cost-to-design review

RERA registration & local approvals

RERA registration and local building permissions are filed and tracked to clearance. No marketing begins until RERA approval is in hand — this is a legal requirement, not a choice.

Led by: Compliance team (CA/CS)

EDITH's role

  • RERA filing & tracking
  • BRS/layout approval
  • NOC coordination

Site engineering & vendor lock-in

The construction schedule is locked, vendors are tied up, and the engineer partner is assigned before the first foundation is dug. No site starts without a complete plan.

Led by: Civil engineer partner

EDITH's role

  • Engineer partner assignment
  • Vendor tie-ups
  • Construction schedule

Construction & site supervision

Structure, MEP, and finishing proceed in stages with site supervision reporting against plan. Progress updates go to investors and JV partners on the agreed cadence.

Led by: Site supervision team

EDITH's role

  • Weekly progress reporting
  • Quality supervision
  • Investor updates

Marketing & agent activation

Listings go live on the Property Marketplace and through the agent network once RERA approval is confirmed. Only verified, RERA-registered projects are publicly marketed.

Led by: Sales & agent network

EDITH's role

  • RERA-compliant marketing
  • Agent network activation
  • Marketplace listing

Bookings, agreements & payment schedules

Sale agreements are structured by the CA/CS team. Payment schedules are tied to construction milestones — not arbitrary deadlines. Every agreement is in writing before any payment is made.

Led by: Sales & legal team

EDITH's role

  • Agreement structuring
  • Milestone-linked payments
  • Compliance oversight

Handover, documentation & post-handover

Units are handed over with completion certificates and documentation. The project moves into post-handover support, and investor returns or flat transfers are processed per the signed agreement.

Led by: Handover & support team

EDITH's role

  • Completion certificate
  • Flat transfers
  • Investor settlements
